Privacy Policy

Rotely · Last updated September 17, 2026

Rotely is an inventory and ordering assistant for bars, operated by Jake Fritzsche as a sole proprietor under the name Rotely ("Rotely," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us: your name, mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Business data you or your business share with us so the service can work: inventory counts, supplier invoices, and point-of-sale sales summaries.
  • Message records: the text messages sent to and received from your number through the service, including the date and time.

How we use it

  • To send you the messages you signed up for: inventory summaries, low-stock alerts, and draft supplier orders.
  • To respond to your replies (for example, confirming or declining an order).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.

SMS / text messaging

You opt in to text messages from Rotely by texting START to our number after agreeing in person to use the service. Message frequency varies (typically a few messages per week). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out; reply HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run Rotely (for example, our text-messaging carrier and cloud hosting provider), and only as needed to deliver the service. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and your rights

We keep your information for as long as your business uses Rotely. If you stop using the service, you can ask us to export or delete your data and we will do so. To make a request, or for any question about this policy, contact Jake Fritzsche at jakefritzsche@yahoo.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
