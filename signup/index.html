Get Rotely inventory texts

Rotely is an inventory and ordering assistant for bars, operated by Jake Fritzsche as a sole proprietor under the name Rotely. If you're a bar owner who has set up Rotely with us, this is how you turn on text messages.

Text START to (325) 221-4683

You'll get a confirmation text right away. That's it — you're enrolled.

What you'll receive

  • A short inventory summary on Monday mornings
  • Low-stock alerts when something's about to run out
  • Draft supplier orders you confirm or decline by replying one word

By texting START you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Rotely at the number you text from. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
